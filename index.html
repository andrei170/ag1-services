08 — FAQ

The questions operators ask before they book.

Will the “rank #1 in 60 days” promise hold for my business?
For commercial-intent keywords with reasonable competition: yes. For ultra-competitive head terms (“car insurance”, “crm software”), no — nobody honestly promises that. We benchmark on the call against your reality. The keywords we target are the ones we’re ranking inside 60 days; we’ll tell you which ones those are before you sign.
Will the “less than 14 days” install hold?
If you have an offer, content, and brand assets ready, yes. If you’re still figuring out positioning or have no audience, we add a 7-day pre-engagement to lock that in — kickoff happens before day-one billing so the clock starts when we both agree the foundation is set.
Can you replicate the $172K-month-one number for me?
No promises. That number depends on the client’s audience, offer, and prior funnel state. What we promise is the install — the working stack, on time, to the AG1 quality bar. The rest is your offer and your audience.
Why pair website + SEO instead of just one?
SEO without a converting site loses the traffic. A converting site without SEO leaves the lead-gen channel dormant. The two compound — the site is the conversion engine; the SEO layer is the qualified-lead engine. Most operators we audit have both half-built and neither working.
Is this safe for a regulated industry / NDA-bound operator?
Yes. Compliance-aware copy is part of the install for licensed operators (CFP, RIA, MD, attorney). We’ve worked with NDA-bound clients before — both partnerships referenced on this page are NDA-bound. We don’t name clients on a sales page; we walk receipts on the call under mutual NDA if needed.
What does the discovery call look like?
20 minutes. Your funnel state, your numbers, your team, your offer. We tell you what we’d ship in the first 14 days and what we’d skip. No deck, no pressure. You leave with a 3-bottleneck audit even if you don’t engage.
