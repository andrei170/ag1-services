07 — FAQ

The seven questions operators ask before they book.

Will the “less than 14 days” promise hold for my business?
If you have an offer, content, and brand assets ready, yes. If you’re still figuring out positioning or have no audience, we add a 7-day pre-engagement to lock that in — kickoff happens before day-one billing so the clock starts when we both agree the foundation is set.
Can you replicate the $172K-month-one number for me?
No promises. That number depends on the client’s audience, offer, and prior funnel state. We benchmark on the discovery call against your reality. What we promise is the install — the working stack, on time, to the AG1 quality bar. The rest is your offer and your audience.
Why pair website + SEO instead of just one?
SEO without a converting site loses the traffic. A converting site without SEO leaves the lead-gen channel dormant. The two compound — the site is the conversion engine; the SEO layer is the qualified-lead engine. Most operators we audit have both half-built and neither working.
Do you handle paid ads?
Capability exists; not the lead service in this engagement. Most operators we work with are organic-first. We layer paid ads when the funnel is converting and the offer is locked — otherwise paid traffic burns money on a leaky funnel.
Is this safe for an operator under NDA / regulated industry?
Yes. Compliance-aware copy is part of the install for licensed operators (CFP, RIA, MD, attorney). We’ve worked with NDA-bound clients before — the $172K-month-one client is one of them. We don’t name clients on a sales page; we walk receipts on the call under mutual NDA if needed.
What does the discovery call look like?
20 minutes. Your funnel state, your numbers, your team, your offer. We tell you what we’d ship in the first 14 days and what we’d skip. No deck, no pressure. You leave with a 3-bottleneck audit even if you don’t engage.
How do payments work?
Wire transfer or Whop invoicing. PIF or split-pay (2-3 payments). International operators welcome. Pricing finalised on the call — the indicative tiers above are starting points, not fixed quotes.
